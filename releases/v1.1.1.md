# ScriptForge v1.1.1

This patch fixes two editing and export bugs:

- **Unicode PDF export:** embedded Unicode fallback fonts now support additional accented text, combining accents, Greek letters, and common symbols such as ✓, →, and ♥. Font advances, measurement and pagination are shared with the editor. Exported text stays selectable and searchable, and screenplay/Fountain content is unchanged. Characters outside the bundled fonts' coverage produce a precise character, code-point and location message instead of a generic failure.
- **Character-cue Tab:** pressing Tab on an Action line such as `VINCENT` or `VINCENT (V.O.)` formats that existing line as Character, inserts one Parenthetical below it, and places the caret between its parentheses. Undo/redo, Shift+Tab and ordinary Action text retain their established behavior.

Writing Sessions, Table Reads, Piper voices, PDF layout controls, native saving and signed updates are preserved. The browser edition includes the same fixes at https://wdk08.github.io/ScriptForge/.

Choose the Apple Silicon or Intel DMG for macOS, or the Windows x64 installer. Existing v1.1.0 installations can use **Check for Updates**. Updater packages are cryptographically signed and verified.

macOS installers remain ad hoc signed and unnotarized; Windows installers have no Authenticode publisher signature. Gatekeeper/SmartScreen/Smart App Control warnings or restrictions may apply. See [INSTALLING.md](https://github.com/wdk08/ScriptForge/blob/v1.1.1/INSTALLING.md).
